Match sim

Pick any two current clubs, across leagues if you like, and play it out. Goals come from a Poisson draw on the matchup's expected goals; each goes to a player weighted by npxG per 90 and minutes share.

Streak

Pick a real team and season, then call each league match as a win, draw or loss. After each call you see the result and what a model fitted only on earlier matches predicted. One wrong call ends the run.

Track record

Every refresh freezes pre-match odds from two models in an append-only ledger. Once a match is played they're scored against the result and the bookmakers' closing and opening lines; lower log-loss is better. Calibration checks whether 60% really happens 60% of the time. The full ledger is in the repo, one row per frozen prediction.
