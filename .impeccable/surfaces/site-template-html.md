Scope: the whole xValue site (site/template.html, published to docs/index.html). Mode: Persuade on the landing (hero, stars window, proof); the tabs below behave as Operate views.
Audience: casual football fans and non-technical recruiters equally; technical proof one click away, never in front of the visitor. Job: be impressed, then believe it. Primary action: open Track Record.
Constraints: premium and grown-up, not kiddie (no chunky rounded type, no toy colours, no bouncy motion); no teletext, no block graphics; no stats on the first screen; startup screen kept and skippable every visit; games and Fermin egg stay in the tabs; must not read as a betting site; the reference's visual language only, never its name, logo, copy or imagery.

## Direction contract

THESIS: xValue as a cinematic night-match broadcast of a model: a floodlit tunnel arch opens the page with blackletter terrace lettering, and the proof is the product itself shown in dark app windows. It refuses the neon analytics dashboard and the cartoon fan site.

OWN-WORLD: near-black ground (#08090a) with a faint grid and scattered points of light; one arched tunnel frame lit cold blue from the left and ember from the right, a floodlit striped pitch receding at its foot. Display: blackletter for the hero words and wordmark, an italic and small-caps Garamond for the linking words, Geist for everything else, tabular figures for data. Panels are #121317 windows with 1px hairline borders and soft depth; one ember action colour (#b8462a); data in cool blue with ember for the line that matters.

STORY: the visitor walks up the tunnel ("Every Player has a Price"), sees the ten most expensive players priced in a real app window, believes it from one held-out sentence beside a scatter of model value against price, and presses the ember Track Record button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: black page, small xValue wordmark top left (italic Garamond x, blackletter Value); centred arch about 80% of the viewport tall, rim lit blue to ember, pitch glowing at its floor; inside it, stacked: italic "Every", blackletter "Player", small caps "HAS A", blackletter "Price"; one plain sentence; the ember "See the track record" button with a quiet "Meet the stars" text link under it. No figures.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "make it something like this: https://getfactura.app/". Signature interaction (busybox.io blend): the startup counts to 100% on a crosshair while strands of blue and ember light fall behind it; the wordmark then flies to its corner and the arch opens out of the horizontal line, title lines resolving from blur. Afterwards light keeps running along the crosshair, the strands keep flowing behind the landing and stop once the tabs are in use, and the stars rows and proof dots arrive in sequence. Motion grammar: slow exponential ease-out, light and focus rather than movement, no bounce, still under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
